# Hi, I’m Yixiao Liu (刘一笑)

I’m an M.S.E. student in Mechanical Engineering and Applied Mechanics at the University of Pennsylvania. I received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I study how agents make decisions together when they have different goals, and how to evaluate whether their plans lead to useful outcomes. I’m also interested in predictive world models, robot learning, and the role of perception and control in executing those plans.

[Website](https://yixiaoliu.me/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4sZTE1oAAAAJ&hl=en) · [Email](mailto:yxlewis@engineering.upenn.edu)

## Research

- **[Spontaneous Cooperation in Language Agents](https://yixiaoliu.me/projects/agent-cooperation/)** (ongoing): Building environments where agents with private goals can find partners, negotiate, and act. I study when completing a deal actually benefits both sides.
- **[UniForget: Concept Unlearning in Unified Multimodal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04467)** (preprint, co-author): We evaluate whether forgetting a concept in image generation also makes it harder to recognize, and what capabilities are lost in the process.

## Selected robotics projects

- **[Motion Planning and Optimal Control](https://github.com/DasVade/Optimal-Control):** Constrained MPC and clipped LQR for a simulated quadrotor, plus minimum-snap trajectory planning.
- **[UR16e Motion Planning and Control](https://github.com/DasVade/UR16e-Robot-Dynamics):** Collision-checked RRT planning, smooth joint trajectories, and torque control in simulation.
- **[3D Reconstruction and Neural Rendering](https://github.com/DasVade/3d-reconstruction-neural-rendering):** Calibrated stereo reconstruction and NeRF evaluation on held-out views.
